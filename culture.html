WHAT IT'S LIKE TO WORK AT WYNK

Work to Live.
Build to Matter.

We believe extraordinary work and a full life aren't mutually exclusive. We expect exceptional work, not exceptional hours.

Making Healthcare Human Again.

Technology is becoming ubiquitous and seamless. We believe the best intelligence fades into the background, freeing healthcare professionals from screens so they can focus on people.

Just Intelligence. Human insight and intelligent technology working together. We build systems that serve people, not the other way around.

Our five core values

01. Be More Human

People come first. We build technology that creates more room for listening, connection, and care—and we treat our teammates with that same humanity.

02. Own the Outcome

Autonomy comes with accountability. Take initiative, make decisions, communicate openly, and follow through. Freedom and responsibility go together.

03. Build Leverage

We're systems builders. Automate repetition, simplify complexity, and build things that keep working when we're offline. Make busywork unnecessary.

04. Intensity Over Hours

We work to live, not live to work. Bring focus, urgency, and ambition. We measure impact, not hours in front of a computer.

05. Don't Be a Jerk

Leave your ego at the door. Nobody has all the answers, and nobody is above helping a teammate. Challenge ideas respectfully, share credit, raise each other up, and win together. Brilliant jerks aren't our thing.

Work intensely. Own the outcome. Build intelligently. Live fully.
