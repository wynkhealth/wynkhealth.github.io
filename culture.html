01 / Our belief

Making Healthcare
Human Again

Systems should serve people.
Not the other way around.

Healthcare professionals should have more room to listen, connect, and care. We believe the best intelligence works quietly in the background, supporting human insight and clinical judgment.

That belief shapes how we build: start with real care workflows, listen to the people using them, and make every improvement count.

02 / The way we work

Work to Live.
Build to Matter.

Extraordinary work and a full life belong together.

We expect exceptional work,
not exceptional hours.

We bring focus, urgency, and high standards. We measure our work by its impact, and build systems that make busywork unnecessary.

For care teams, that means a partner who respects their time and focuses on meaningful improvements to their day.

03 / Our values in practice

How belief becomes behavior.

The standards we hold ourselves to—and what they mean for the people we serve.

01

Be More Human

People come first. Listen with curiosity, respect the person in front of you, and make more room for connection.

For care teams: technology shaped around people’s needs.

02

Own the Outcome

Take initiative, communicate openly, and follow through. Freedom and responsibility go together.

For care teams: a team that listens and takes responsibility.

03

Build Leverage

Simplify complexity. Automate repetition. Build systems that keep working so people don’t have to do the same work twice.

For care teams: a focus on reducing everyday friction.

04

Intensity Over Hours

Bring focus and ambition. Measure impact, not time at a computer. Make space for life beyond work.

For care teams: improvements that respect their time.

05

Don’t Be a Jerk

Leave ego at the door. Challenge ideas respectfully, share credit, help teammates improve, and win together. High standards work best with humility.

For care teams: collaboration grounded in respect.

What won’t change

We want to win.
Just not at any cost.

We won’t sacrifice our principles, our people, or the trust of healthcare teams just to grow faster.
